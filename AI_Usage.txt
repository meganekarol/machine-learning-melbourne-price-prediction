AI Tools Usage

1) Tools Used

We used ChatGPT (OpenAI GPT-5) and other online resources (official documentation, GitHub examples, and Stack Overflow discussions) as intelligent assistants throughout our Machine Learning Lifecycle project.
These tools provided support for code structure, debugging, documentation, and conceptual understanding, while all final decisions and implementations were made by our project team.

2) Purpose of AI Assistance

AI tools were used to support development efficiency and learning, not to replace human reasoning or experimentation.
Specifically, AI tools helped us with the following:

⦁	Feature selection understanding and comparison:
Helped us explain and compare various feature selection techniques such as:
VarianceThreshold (filtering low-variance features)
RFECV (recursive feature elimination with cross-validation)
ANOVA F-test (statistical relevance of features)
Mutual Information (nonlinear dependency measure):
The AI also helped interpret why some techniques (like RFECV and VarianceThreshold) performed more consistently across our models.

⦁	Pipeline design improvements:
Suggested enhancements for handling mixed data types (categorical and numerical) within a single preprocessing pipeline using ColumnTransformer, OneHotEncoder, StandardScaler, and custom imputers.

⦁	Error diagnosis and debugging:
Provided detailed explanations and solutions for common implementation issues, such as:
Handling missing data with SimpleImputer and DataFrameImputer.
Resolving compatibility errors related to get_feature_names_out().
Fixing instability in Gradient Boosting by adjusting learning rates, max depths, and number of estimators.

⦁	App integration:
Explained the roles and interaction between app.py (API interface) and export_model.py (model saving and loading).
This helped us understand how the trained pipeline and model artifact integrate with the deployed API for prediction.

⦁   Evaluation and interpretation:
Guided us in interpreting evaluation metrics (R², MAE, RMSE, MAPE) and in visualizing diagnostic plots such as residuals, prediction errors, and feature importances.

⦁	Documentation and explanation quality:
Helped us draft clear markdown explanations, structure project justifications, and ensure our notebooks followed a professional and logical narrative flow.

3) How AI Specifically Helped

⦁	Identified causes of overflow errors in Gradient Boosting and proposed safer hyperparameter ranges (e.g., reduced learning rate, smaller tree depth).
⦁	Assisted in integrating imputers and custom transformers (DataFrameImputer, FrequencyEncoder) for a consistent and leak-free pipeline.
⦁	Explained why VarianceThreshold and RFECV gave the most stable feature selection results across multiple models.
⦁	Provided code structure templates for unified preprocessing and feature selection pipelines.
⦁	Suggested designs for model comparison visuals (bar plots for R², RMSE) and interpretation of cross-validated results.
⦁	Helped clarify how to back-transform log predictions (from Log_Price to Price) for interpretable evaluation metrics

4) Our Contribution
All AI-generated code and text were:
⦁	Reviewed, tested, and understood by the team before inclusion.
⦁	Modified and adapted to fit our dataset, project structure, and intended explanations.
⦁	Interpreted and justified in our own words during analysis and documentation.

⦁	We mutually decided which models, parameters, and evaluation methods to use
⦁	All conclusions and justification sections were based on my own interpretation
